Star Glow

Connect an image to Source and choose the Alpha, Luminance, RGB, or Brightness channel to select highlights. Threshold removes dim values; Fade Out softens the threshold for Luminance and Brightness. Brightness Gain scales the selected light. Source Opacity and Glow Opacity control their contributions.

Lengths and Highlight Radius are measured on the effect's world pixel grid. The source is rendered into that grid before the glow is calculated, including raster-level DPI placement. Vertical, Horizontal and Diagonal Lengths control independent streaks. Each direction has its own color and optional editable ramp; the ramp runs from the streak center to its ends. Diagonal creates two crossing directions. Glow Rotation turns all streaks. Highlight Radius adds a two-axis glow in Highlight Color. A zero length or radius disables that contribution.

Box, Approximated Gaussian, Gaussian and Exponential select distinct finite-support profiles. Approximated Gaussian uses a quadratic B-spline; Gaussian and Exponential weights end at the chosen length. Approximated Input averages aligned 2-by-2 input blocks before filtering. Add sums the source and glow; Premultiply composites the glow over the source using glow coverage.

The controls, ramps and colors can be animated and saved in scenes and presets. Select a ramp stop and enter precise R, G, B and A values below the ramp; RGB accepts finite HDR values and alpha ranges from 0 to 1. Float processing preserves those values. Partial transparency participates in highlight extraction and compositing.
