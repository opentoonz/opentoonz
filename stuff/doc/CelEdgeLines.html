Cel Edge Lines

Draw lines along the boundaries between flat colour regions. Add Cel Edge Lines from the Stylize category and connect the drawing to Source. It suits lineless styles that still need some outlines, and re-inking a drawing after its colours changed.

By default there is a black line of weight 2 between any two colours, and one around the drawing where it meets transparency.

Rules

Each rule names the two regions it draws a line between, and the line's weight and colour. An end of a rule is Any Colour (every opaque region), Colour (regions of that colour, within Colour Match Tolerance), or Transparent (where the drawing ends). The order of the two ends does not matter. Where several rules match a boundary, the first one decides its line; where none matches, there is no line. Up to 8 rules; Rules sets how many are shown and used.

Line Weight is the line's total width, centred on the boundary, in the drawing's own pixels; lines around the drawing reach half their weight outside it. Fractional weights are antialiased. Line Colour's alpha is the line's opacity.

Regions

A pixel belongs to a flat region when most of its neighbours have its colour, within Region Tolerance (per channel, from 0 to 1). Raise it for noisy or dithered colours; lower it to separate close colours. The antialiased pixels between two regions are split between them, up to Antialias Gap pixels from a flat region. Areas that are not flat, such as gradients, form no region and get no lines.

Output

Lines over Source composites the lines over the drawing. Lines Only outputs the lines alone, for compositing elsewhere. Line Opacity scales every line.

Notes

Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported. Lines are computed in the level's own pixels for raster levels, so they keep their weight at any preview size; the result does not depend on how the frame is split into render tiles. Boundaries fall between pixels, so a very thin line on a diagonal edge shows the pixel steps.
