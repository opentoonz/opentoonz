Cel Boil

Add a controlled hand-drawn "boil" to clean artwork, so a held drawing does not look frozen. Add Cel Boil from the Distort category and connect the drawing to Source. The image, or only its lines, is displaced by a smooth wobble that holds for a number of frames and then changes.

Boil

Whole Image moves every pixel. Lines Only moves only the lines: pixels darker than Line Darkness (0 is black, 1 is white), with their antialiased edge. The paint under a moving line is filled from the nearest paint, so the lines move over the colours without opening holes.

Shape

Amplitude is the largest displacement, in the drawing's own pixels; 1 to 2 gives a subtle boil. Wobble Size is the size of the wobble's features, from 1 to 100 pixels: small values make the lines crawl, large values make them sway. Detail adds a finer wobble on top, as a share of the amplitude. An amplitude of 0 passes the image through exactly.

Timing

Cycle is the number of frames each wobble is held: 2 is the classic boil on twos. Blend is the share of each cycle spent changing smoothly into the next wobble; 0 jumps. Seed picks the sequence of wobbles: the same seed always gives the same boil, so renders are repeatable, and two columns with different seeds boil independently.

Notes

Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported. The wobble is tied to the drawing's position, not to the render tiles or the preview size, and the frames of one cycle share a cache entry.
