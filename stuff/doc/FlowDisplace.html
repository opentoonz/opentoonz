Flow Displace

Warp a drawing with a second image, for the secondary motion of capes, hair and water without redrawing. Add Flow Displace from the Distort category and connect the drawing to Source, the image that drives the warp to Displacement, and optionally a matte to Matte.

Displacement

Vector Field reads the Displacement image as motion: red is the horizontal motion and green the vertical, with 0.5 (mid grey) meaning none, as the Motion Flow FX writes them. Paint or generate it in another column; an animated field animates the warp. Grey Map reads its brightness instead: white pushes along Direction, black pushes the opposite way, mid grey not at all. Strength is the motion of a full-scale value in pixels at 100%; Invert reverses it. Where the Displacement image is transparent there is no motion.

Matte

When a matte is connected, its alpha weights the motion: nothing moves outside it, everything inside it, and soft edges blend between. Use it to keep a body still while its cape moves.

Secondary motion

Noise Amplitude adds a smooth noise motion of that many pixels, which drifts over time at Noise Speed (noise planes per frame). With a matte and no Displacement image, this alone makes hair or water ripple. Noise Size is the size of its features and Seed picks its pattern; renders are repeatable.

Every value can be animated. 8-bit, 16-bit and floating-point images are supported, and the result does not depend on render tiles.
